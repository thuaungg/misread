**1. Welcome Back to School**
We hope you and your child/ward had the opportunity to bond and share meaningful
experiences together during the June holidays. As we begin Term 3, we would like to

continue to strive to “ **Lead Well, Teach Well, Learn Well and Grow Well** ” as a community.

**2. A Kaleidoscope of Culture, Art and Learning (Term 2)**
Over the ten weeks of Term 2, the school engaged in a vibrant mix of cultural, artistic and
educational experiences. Students were immersed in the arts and heritage through our
aesthetics programme, cultural performances and learning journeys.
Their global and local perspectives were broadened through International Friendship Day,
the enriching P5 Overseas Learning Experience in Penang, and our Hari Raya concert.
Active and experiential learning also took centre stage through adventure camps, the
National School Games, and participation in a meaningful Values-in-Action activity with our
community partners.
Our Choir and Dance groups also proudly represented the school at the Singapore Youth
Festival Arts Presentation. These collective experiences played an important role in
strengthening school identity and nurturing a sense of belonging.

**4. Term 3 GIVER3S Values – Graciousness and Versatility**
Our school curriculum seeks to equip our students with 21st Century Competencies such
as Critical, Adaptive and Inventive Thinking. The value of “Graciousness” reminds our
students of the importance of being kind in their interactions with others, both in word and
action. Similarly, the value of “Versatility” encourages our students to embrace change and
equips them with the skills to navigate life’s challenges in a volatile world. We seek the
support of parents/guardians in continuing to be exemplary role models for your child/ward
and in working hand in hand with us to guide your child/ward towards becoming the best
version of themselves.

**7. Home-Based Learning (HBL) for P1 to P5**
Primary 1 to 5 students will have **Home-Based Learning (HBL)** during the two days of
PSLE Oral Examinations. This helps them take ownership of their learning, manage
their time responsibly, and build confidence in independent study. Online
assignments will be provided to familiarise students with online learning and help them
maintain healthy screen-time habits (see page 38 of the Students’ Handbook).

Please encourage your child/ward to:
• Monitor progress: Check off completed tasks according to the school’s curriculum
schedule.
• Practise time management: Work first, then play. Balance study, rest and play.

**8. Bags of Love – Food Donation Drive**
Our annual food donation drive, “Bags of Love”, will run from Monday, 6 July to Friday 10
July. Over these three weeks, the school will be collecting food items for our charity partner,
Food from the Heart, as well as for our operations staff. More information will be shared via
Parents Gateway (PG), and we look forward to your support for this meaningful cause.

**9. Activating Learning Spaces as a Third Teacher**
In the coming months, we will progressively enhance selected learning spaces around the
school to make learning even more engaging and meaningful for our students. We believe
that the environment can serve as a “third teacher” — alongside teachers and classroom
lessons, thoughtfully designed spaces can spark curiosity, encourage conversation,
support reflection and help children learn through authentic experiences. Through these
spaces, we hope to nurture our students’ confidence, creativity, collaboration and sense of
belonging, while making school an even more welcoming and inspiring place to learn.
