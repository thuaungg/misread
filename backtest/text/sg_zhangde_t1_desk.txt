**1. Happy 2026 to all parents/ guardians and students of Zhangde Primary!**

**2. Goodbye & Welcome to our Principals**
This year, we bid a fond farewell to Mrs Rita Dass, who has served as our Principal from 2019
to 2025. Her strong and strategic leadership, and unwavering commitment and dedication to
our students' growth have been invaluable. Her sterling contributions to ZPS would not be
forgotten and we wish her happy retirement.

At the same time, we are delighted to welcome Mr Kyaw Saw Lynn as our new Principal. Mr

Please join us in expressing our gratitude to Mrs Rita Dass and extending a warm welcome to

**3. 2025 PSLE Results**
We are happy to share that 95.9% of our Primary 6 students have been successfully posted to
Secondary Schools. Additionally, 17 of our students were successful in their Direct School
Admissions (DSA) application. The hard work of our students has paid off, and they could not
have done that without the dedication of our teachers, and the steadfast support of our parents.
We are immensely proud of their achievements and wish them all the best in their future
endeavours. Congratulations to our Class of 2025! Your journey at ZPS has come to an end,
but we are confident that the GIVER 3 S values and lifeskills you have learnt will serve you well
in the secondary schools.

**4 . School’s Vision, Mission, Values and 2026 Theme (** Reference: Parent Handbook 2026)
Our vision of "Learning School, GIVER 3 S Heart" encapsulates our commitment to fostering
both academic excellence and a spirit of generosity and compassion. We believe that true
education goes beyond textbooks and the school walls. At ZPS, we aim to nurture students
who are thinking individuals and gracious citizens. We hope that Zhangderens will grow to be
lifelong learners who make a positive impact on the world around them, firmly anchored on ZPS
GIVER 3 S Heart (Graciousness, Integrity, Versatility, Empathy, Responsibility, Respect,

values at home for reinforcement. In 2026, under the theme of “ **Curious Hearts, Connected Minds — Growing the Future Together** ”, we aim to reinforce a culture of inquiry, a sense of
belonging, and our collective purpose for educating the young for the future.

**7. Refreshed Guidelines for Home-School Partnership**
We believe in building strong partnerships with our parents and community partners to support
the holistic development of Zhangderens so that they will become thinking individuals and
gracious citizens. We look forward to working with you to build a strong foundation of
knowledge, skills, and values in Zhangderens in a safe, positive, and conducive learning
environment. MOE has come up with refreshed guidelines for home-school partnerships which
provides greater clarity on how schools and parents can work together.
A summary is highlighted below:

**A. Respectful Communication**
• Listen to and understand each other’s perspectives and concerns regarding each child.
• Communicate kindly using official channels. Teachers are not required to share their
personal mobile numbers.
• Respect each other’s time by communicating during working hours.

Page **2** of **6 B. Role Models**
• Practise gratitude and find joy in everyday experiences with our children.
• Instil confidence by encouraging responsibility and believing in each child’s abilities.
• Model good values in words and actions, at school and at home.
**C. Real Connections**
• Build strong bonds through shared experiences and meaningful conversations.
• Establish good habits for our children to stay confident and in control of their technology
use.
• Provide a balanced mix of engaging online and offline activities, at school and at home.

**11. Character and Citizenship Education (** Reference: Parent Handbook 2026)
As we begin the new school year in 2026, we reaffirm our commitment to nurture strong values
and clear expectations in our students. In Term 1, our school-wide focus will be on
**Responsibility** and **Resilience** , two foundational values that shape both character and
learning. Responsibility encourages our students to take ownership of their learning, behaviour,
and choices, while resilience equips them with the perseverance and confidence to navigate
challenges, learn from mistakes, and grow stronger through setbacks. These values will be
intentionally woven into our daily routines, classroom practices, and learning experiences,
guiding students to make positive choices and develop a strong sense of self-discipline. We
value the partnership with parents and guardians and encourage you to support this focus at
home by entrusting your children with age-appropriate responsibilities and affirming their efforts
as they learn to overcome difficulties. Together, as a school family, we can set clear
expectations and nurture resilient, responsible learners who are ready to thrive in the year
ahead and beyond.

**16. Road Safety and Orderliness (** Reference: Parent Handbook 2026)
We would like to seek your cooperation to work with the school in ensuring the safety of
students coming to and leaving school. This is especially important as young children may not
have the skills required to use roads properly and do not always fully appreciate or anticipate
the dangers presented.

The traffic flow is very busy at the start and end of the school day. We appreciate all parents
being gracious and patient, waiting in line and following our ground staff's coordination for the
safety of our students and staff.
Start of School Day:
• Parents driving to school to drop off children in the mornings, please remain in your
vehicles. Move off swiftly once your children have alighted.

• Observe speed limits and remain vigilant for pedestrians within the school zone. Please
follow directions from traffic marshals to facilitate safe and smooth traffic flow.
End of School Day:
• Parents waiting to fetch children home during peak hours are strongly advised to park at
nearby HDB car parks.
Dismissal Arrangements:
1. Gate 3: For dismissal of P1, P2 and P3 students
o Only parents of P1, P2, P3 students will be allowed into the limited Parents'
Waiting Area.
o Please do not bring pets into the parents' waiting area.
2. Gate 2: For dismissal of all other students
We kindly remind all parents and guardians that these measures are in place to ensure the
safety and smooth flow of traffic around our school. Your cooperation is greatly appreciated.

If your child must cycle to school, he/she should wear a protective helmet, demonstrate
competency in safe cycling and is aware of the cycling rules. Please inform the Form Teacher if
your child is cycling to school.
