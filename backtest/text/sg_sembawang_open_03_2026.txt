**Important Information… Safety, Emergency Preparedness & Student Well-Being**

4. Attendance and Temperature Taking Exercise (ATTEx) 2026. A school-wide ATTEx
was carried out on 1 April to maintain the school's preparedness to reactivate the temperature-
taking regime, necessary in the event of pandemic where checking of temperature is critical.
We have requested teachers to follow-up with students without an Oral Digital Thermometer
(ODT) or who have presented an unserviceable ODT during the exercise. History has
reminded us on the necessity and importance of having the capability (i.e. the thermometer)
to measure temperature as there could be shortages of this commodity during a pandemic.
We urge parents to ensure that your child is equipped with a serviceable ODT. Thank you,
parents.

6. Crow Situation at the Canteen. Following the advice and guidance by NParks official
who visited the school at the onset of the crow situation in the school canteen, we have
implemented physical measures such as display of CDs and crow effigies (See Figure 2) as
deterrent efforts to keep the crows away. In pursuit of greater success, we continued to
engage NParks on other deterrent measures such as the use of audio devices and seeking
NParks to influence the culling of crows at our region, while continuing educating our students
on proper dining etiquette.

14. Punctuality. Students who are not seated at their designated assembly areas by 7.30
a.m. will be considered late. Students who are late will miss crucial moments where teachers
introduce key concepts and set expectations for the day's activities. Late joiners in class may
experience struggles to catch up with instructions and/or feel anxious about participating in
ongoing activities. The school seeks continued partnership with parents to ensure that all
children arrive punctually for school.
