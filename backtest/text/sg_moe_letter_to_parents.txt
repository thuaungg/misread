**INFORMATION ON THE GIFTED EDUCATION PROGRAMME**

The Gifted Education Programme (GEP) seeks to develop the intellectually gifted to
their full potential in an educational environment that is designed to stimulate intellectual
growth. An equally important emphasis of the GEP is the development of social responsibility
and sound moral values in the pupils.

Pupils who have been identified for the GEP will attend classes from Primary 4 to
Primary 6 in one of nine primary schools: Anglo-Chinese School (Primary) and Catholic High
(Primary) for boys; Raffles Girls' Primary for girls; Henry Park Primary, Rosyth, Nan Hua
Primary, Nanyang Primary, St. Hilda’s Primary, and Tao Nan for both boys and girls.

The identification exercise involves two stages:

a. Screening Stage: The Screening exercise will be carried out by the school on **15 August 2024** and all Primary 3 pupils enrolled in a Singapore government or
government-aided school are invited to take part. Pupils will be asked to work on
some English Language and Mathematics questions based on what they would
have learnt in the syllabuses from Primary 1 to Primary 3. Both multiple-choice
and open-ended questions are included and the format is similar to that of
Mathematics and English Language questions assigned in school.

b. Selection Stage: Pupils who perform well in the GEP Screening exercise will be
invited to participate in the GEP Selection exercise (on **15 and 16 October 2024** ),
which is made up of 3 papers – English Language, Mathematics and General
Ability. The English Language and Mathematics papers will assess what pupils
would have learnt from Primary 1 to Primary 3 in the English Language and
Mathematics syllabuses. The General Ability paper is an assessment of logical
thinking and general reasoning ability.

The outcome of the GEP Selection Exercise will be released to all pupils through
the school in early **November 2024.** Selected pupils will be invited to join the
Primary 4 GEP.

The GEP is an enrichment programme pitched at the pupils’ intellectual ability. The
subjects that are enriched in the Primary GEP are English Language, Mathematics, Science,
Social Studies, and Character & Citizenship Education. Pupils who are proficient in their
Mother Tongue language will be given the option to offer the subject at the Higher Mother
Tongue level. At the end of Primary 6, GEP pupils sit the Primary School Leaving Examination
(PSLE) together with pupils in the mainstream.

At the secondary level, provisions for GEP pupils are made through school-based
gifted education programmes in selected Integrated Programme (IP) schools. The IP schools
which offer school-based gifted education programmes are: Anglo-Chinese School
(Independent), Dunman High School, Raffles Institution, Raffles Girls’ School (Secondary),
Hwa Chong Institution, Nanyang Girls’ High School and the National University of Singapore
High School of Mathematics and Science. GEP pupils admitted to the 6-year IP in these
schools will receive a school-based enriched and challenging curriculum to stretch them
intellectually.

More information on the GEP can be obtained from our website:
