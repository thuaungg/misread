**School Assessment Policy (rephrase)**
19 As your child progresses into Secondary 2, we continue to prioritize a balanced pace for teaching and
learning. In alignment with MOE’s direction, there are **no Mid-Year Examinations (MYE)** . This allows our

**Secondary 4 and 5 Programmes in Term 1 CTSS Future-Ready Day (6 January 2026)**
21 We strongly believe in supporting our students in discovering their interests and strengths whilst
pursuing their aspirations with a strengths-based approach. This year, we have rebranded our annual Career
Festival as the CTSS Future-Ready Day. This shift moves beyond a focus on specific careers to emphasise the
development of essential skills, dispositions, and competencies that will enable our students to navigate and
succeed in an ever-evolving world of work. In a rapidly changing landscape where many future careers may

● **Aspire and Grow**

**Total Defence Week 2026 (9 to 13 February 2026)**
24 The focus for Total Defence Day 2026 “Together We Keep Singapore Strong”, will highlight
Singaporean’s readiness and resilience in the face of crises and challenges. To encourage our students to
develop and value this resilience, we will be participating in the Exercise SG Ready 2026 (ESR26) from 9 to
13 February 2026.

25 Exercise SG Ready (ESR) is a nationwide exercise to strengthen Singapore’s preparedness and
resilience against crises and disruptions. Simulations of possible crises and disruptions that may happen in
Singapore enable students to learn to respond in a calm, resilient and responsible way, look out for each
other, and be prepared for future threats and challenges. It will also be an occasion to rally our students to
Connect and Contribut e through acts of Care . Through the shared experience of possible disruption, students
will have the opportunity to listen to others’ stories, reflect on their own experiences of resilience and
adaptability, and cultivate the Will to Act in order to Contribute positively to their lives and the community
around them. More information on ESR will be shared with parents through Parents Gateway.
