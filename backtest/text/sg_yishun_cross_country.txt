Dear Parents/Guardian

**RE: YSS ANNUAL CROSS-COUNTRY EVENT**

I refer to the "Letter to Parents/Guardian - Term 2" sent on 8 March. As mentioned, the school will be
hosting its annual cross-country on 12 May. The details are as follows:

Date : 12 May 2023
Time : 7:30 - 10:30 am
Venue : Sengkang Hockey Stadium
57 Anchorvale Rd, Singapore 544964

Attire : PE Attire (Class T-shirt and sports shoes are allowed)

Students will be required to report directly to the stadium and have been briefed on how to travel to the
stadium. The school will also station route marshals along the way to assist students. Sengkang Hockey
Stadium can be accessed through these routes:

• Bus 85 from Yishun bus interchange
• 5 mins walk from Farmway LRT Station (SW2)

We would like to take this opportunity to cordially invite you to join us in the day's festivities as a spectator
or as a runner in the 3km Staff & Parents' Race along the scenic Sengkang Riverside Park.

We thank you for your support as we celebrate the end of the school term. As this is a school event,
students may only be absent with a valid reason. Students are required to report to the stadium
regardless of the weather condition. In the event of wet weather, students will be dismissed from the
stadium.

If you have any enquiries or should you want to take part in the staff and parents' race, please contact Mr
Aidil at 6876 7129. Thank you.

Mr Aidil Idris
HOD PE & CCA
