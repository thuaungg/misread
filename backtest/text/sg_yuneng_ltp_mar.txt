**2. UPCOMING EVENTS 2.1 Singapore Youth Festival (SYF)**

The Ministry of Education (MOE) organises the Singapore Youth Festival (SYF) annually to
celebrate the vitality and talents of our youth in the arts. Beyond showcasing artistic excellence,
SYF serves as a meaningful platform for the development of character, 21st century
competencies and social-emotional learning through the arts. It encourages mass participation
in arts co-curricular activities, where students experience the joy of the arts both as CCA
members and as appreciative audience members at performances and exhibitions. The SYF
platforms also support nation-building efforts by nurturing cultural awareness and deepening
appreciation for Singapore’s diverse heritage through aesthetics education.

The SYF Arts Presentation is a performance and benchmarking platform that provides valuable
opportunities for continuous learning and growth, with professional feedback from industry
practitioners. This year, we are proud that our school’s performing arts CCA groups (Chinese
Orchestra, Choir, Contemporary Dance and Malay Dance) will be participating in the SYF Arts
Presentation. We look forward to celebrating our students’ dedication, teamwork and passion as
they represent our school with pride.

**2.3 Primary 4 SwimSafer Programme**

SwimSafer 2.0 is a national water safety programme designed to equip students with swimming
proficiency and essential water survival skills. In Term 2, our Primary 4 students will participate
in the programme from Tuesday, 24 March 2026 to Wednesday, 13 May 2026. Lessons will be
conducted during curriculum hours (PE periods), and further details will be provided in a separate
letter.

**2.4 Class Photo Taking**

Class photo-taking sessions will be held on Tuesday, 31 March 2026 and Wednesday, 1 April
2026. The schedule for each class will be shared closer to the dates. Students are to be in full
school uniform with ties, even if they have PE lessons on the day of their scheduled session. PE
lessons for affected classes will be conducted in the classroom. Please note that there will be no
make-up session for students who miss the allocated date for the class.

**2.5 FAME@YN**

FAME@YN is a platform for Primary 4 to Primary 6 students to showcase their talents and build
confidence. Students may perform a dance, song, or instrumental piece based on the theme
“ Together We Shine ” and the performances will be held during the respective level recesses in
April 2026.

More details will be shared during Pre Morning Assembly on Monday, 9 March 2026, and following
which the period to indicate interest will be from Monday, 9 March 2026 to Monday, 23 March
2026 via SLS.

We look forward to the lively and engaging performances by our students. Wishing all participants
an enriching experience as they unleash their creativity!

**2.6 Primary 3 Learning Journey to the Zoo**

As part of the Science Department’s efforts to broaden students’ exposure to real -life
applications of science and cultivate scientific inquiry skills, all Primary 3 students will participate
in a learning journey to the Singapore Zoo on Thursday, 2 April 2026.

Students will attend a talk on “Body Covering” and participate in an ICT -infused learning trail. As
part of the P3 Science Explorer Programme, students will also complete a post-programme
project to design animal enclosures. This project reinforces their understanding of major groups
of living organisms and animal habitats, while fostering critical and adaptive thinking skills. An
integrated Education and Career Guidance (ECG) component will also allow students to explore
and appreciate career opportunities within the zoo environment. Further details will be provided
in a separate letter.
