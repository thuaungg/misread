The Math Department runs THINKING@PLAY, a weekly programme that aims
to cultivate students’ creative and critical thinking through engaging Math
games. The programme provides students with opportunities to develop
strategic thinking and problem-solving skills while enjoying meaningful play.
Through puzzles and games such as Hide & Seek and Tantrix, students explore
different ways of thinking and learn to apply logical reasoning in a fun and
interactive environment.

THINKING@PLAY takes place every Friday during recess from 8.30 am to 10.30
am for Primary 1 to Primary 4 students. Students can visit the booths located
next to KidZone 2 to try out the various Math games. At the booths, students
may obtain a stamp card from the parent volunteers and earn a stamp each
time they try a game. Once they have completed the stamp card, they can
redeem a prize, making the experience both rewarding and enjoyable.

## Primary 1 Hi-Lite Programme:
## Walking Through HPPS

Hi-Lite (Holistic Integrated Learning Involving Thematic Experiences) brings learning
to life by weaving together different subjects into fun and meaningful experiences for
our students. Through this approach, everyday curriculum time becomes more
engaging and enjoyable for our young learners.

“A Walk to Remember” is our overarching theme for the year, and our P1 students
began their journey with “Walking Through HPPS”. As part of this experience, students
explored different parts of the school through a series of interactive stations.

At each station, they worked together to complete activities, building teamwork and
collaboration. Along the way, students practised listening and speaking as they
communicated with one another, while also exploring ideas across different subjects.
They observed their surroundings, shared their thoughts, and made sense of their
experiences together.

It was a joyful start to their Hi-Lite journey, filled with laughter, teamwork, and
meaningful learning.

## Quality Learning Experiences

## World Water Day Recess Booth

In commemoration of World Water Day, the school organized an interactive recess booth to raise
awareness about the importance of water conservation and sustainability. The booth served as a
platform for our students to learn that they can make a difference and take on concrete actions in
water conservation at home.

During recess, students participated in hands-on activities designed to deepen their
understanding of water usage in Singapore and the good and bad water habits. Activities included,
“Fishing for Water Droplets” and “Let’s Save Water Wally” where students identified good water
saving habits and use water wisely.

Students were also encouraged to save water, such as taking shorter showers, turning off taps
when not in use, and reusing water whenever possible. Informative posters and videos highlighted
key messages about protecting this precious resource. Through this initiative, we hope that
students gain a greater appreciation for water and are empowered to take small but meaningful
actions in their daily lives.

## Meaningful Partnerships

## HPPA Annual General Meeting 2026:
## Working Together Behind the Scenes

If you’ve ever wondered how school events come together so smoothly, the answer is simple:
they don’t happen by themselves. Behind every event is careful planning by the teachers —
and often, support from parents who step in when extra hands are needed.

At the 27 th HPPA Annual General Meeting (AGM) held on 27 February 2026, parents, school
leaders and staff looked back on an exciting year of working together to support the many
programmes and activities that make school life richer for our children.

In 2025, parent volunteers were busy behind the scenes, clocking over 2,000 hours across
101 activities — from Children’s Day and International Friendship Day to Teachers’ Day, P6
Graduation and weekly storytelling sessions. The AGM also recognised the growing group of
active volunteers in the HPPA Involvement Team (HIT), which was formed to support HPPA’s
work more regularly.

In his address, Principal Mr Charles Chan thanked parents for their steady support of the
school, noting that while teachers drive the programmes and events, the involvement of
parents often makes it possible to do more for the children. He emphasised that the strong
partnership between home and school continues to be an important part of the Henry Park
experience.

The AGM also saw the election of the new HPPA Executive Committee for the 2026–2027
term. With both returning and new members stepping forward to serve, HPPA continues to
rely on parents who are willing to give their time, not just for their own children, but for the
wider school community.

Parents who are keen to be more involved are welcome to join HPPA to support school
activities, stay connected to their children’s school journey, build friendships with fellow
parents and be part of a supportive parenting community. Join us or find out more at
linktr.ee/hppa.sg.
