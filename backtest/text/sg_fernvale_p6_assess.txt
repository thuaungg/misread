3. The details of Weighted Assessment 2 is provided in Annex 1. The details of the next
assessment will be shared at the start of Term 3.

4. We would like to inform parents/guardians of the following:
▪ All absences **must be supported** by a Medical Certificate (MC) and/or certifying letters
from qualified medical practitioners registered with the Singapore Medical Council.
▪ Students who are absent for the assessment without a valid MC will be marked as ‘Absent’
and **will not be awarded any marks** for the paper that they missed. Students who are
absent with an MC will have the marks prorated accordingly.
▪ There will be **no make-up assessment** for absentees.
▪ Students found cheating/attempting to cheat or engaging in acts of dishonesty/disruption
will face disciplinary action and may be given a zero mark for the respective assessment.

5. A soft copy of the assessment schedule is placed in our school website, under Parents’ Corner,
Assessment Matters (https://www.fernvalepri.moe.edu.sg/parents-corner/letters-to-parents/).
