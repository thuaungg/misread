**YSS PE T-SHIRT REFRESH**

Thank you for partnering us as we continue to provide your child/ward with an enriching and
purposeful experience in Yishun Secondary School. This year, we are proud that several of
our sports CCAs have done well, with our volleyball, basketball and football teams achieving
the top four positions in their respective National School Games competitions, and this
success has provided renewed energy among Yishunites.

In the spirit of this newfound vigour and pride, we are happy to inform you that we will be
refreshing the design of the school PE T-shirt. The new PE T-shirt is designed by our student,
**Kiev Sawin Tan Ke Cheng** from **Secondary 4N3** . His design was selected from over 30
submissions received during the School PE T-shirt Design Competition organised by the YSS
Prefectorial Board earlier this year.

The new PE T-shirt can be purchased from the school bookshop at the same price as the
current PE t-shirt. Students can continue to wear the current PE T-shirt. However, from this
week, the school bookshop will only sell the new PE T-shirts.

We hope that this refresh will help Yishunites further distinguish themselves and forge a
greater sense of school identity. I thank you once again for your continued support. Let us
continue to work together to provide a meaningful and holistic learning experience for every
Yishunite.

Regards

Mrs Regina Lee
Principal
