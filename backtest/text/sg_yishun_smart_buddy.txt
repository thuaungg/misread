Dear Parents/ Guardians,

Our school has been working closely with POSB for the past couple of months and we are pleased to
finally announce that we have successfully implemented POSB Smart Buddy as an e-payment option
in our school canteen and bookstore. This is in support of the MOU that MOE has partnered with POSB
to extend Smart Buddy to all schools in Singapore. With this implementation in Yishun Secondary
School, all our students can now opt to use e-payments for their in-school purchases from
24 June 2024 onwards.

The POSB Smart Buddy Programme is the **World’s first in-school wearable tech programme** to enable
a contactless payment ecosystem that helps cultivate sensible saving and spending habits among
children. Paired with the Smart Buddy mobile app, all parents, like yourself, would be able to remotely
manage your child’s daily allowances, track their spending and savings, and even set saving goals in a
digital and interactive way. You can learn more about POSB Smart Buddy and choose to apply here or
watch this video to learn more about its features.

Kindly note that your child will be able to make purchases with the cards/ watch shown below. You can
also download the POSB Smart Buddy mobile app and link these cards/ watch to track your child’s
payments and savings.

**WE SEEK, WE STRIVE, WE SOAR Other Accepted Cards (But cannot be linked on the Smart Buddy mobile app) POSB ATM Card* DBS ATM Card* POSB PAssion Card* Home Team NS PAssion POSB Debit Card* NETS Pre-Paid Card (NPC) NETS FlashPay**

*Must have NETS Contactless logo

In addition, two balance readers are installed in the school canteen where your child can tap his/her
student card to check their card balance.

We hope that in support of this national initiative, our students will be able to experience digital
payments in a safe and secured environment.

Thank you.
