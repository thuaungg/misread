**Home-based Learning for Secondary 1-3 Students on Thu, 11 Jul 2024**

The school will be carrying out Home-based Learning for Secondary 1-3 students on Thu, 11 Jul due
to the need for urgent repairs in some of our classrooms.

Fans in two classrooms had disengaged from the ceiling this afternoon during break. No students were
injured in the incident.

Procedure for HBL

We will follow the usual HBL morning procedure where students log into SLS to report their presence
to their respective Form Teachers. Students who do not have computer or wifi access at home can
report to Comp Lab 1 at 7:30am.

Secondary 4/5 students will continue with physical lessons in school as per normal, in specially
assigned rooms.

CCA

CCA sessions will continue in the afternoon although no classrooms will be used.

Emergency Exercise for Secondary One Students

The Emergency Exercise involving Secondary One students originally planned for Thu, 11 Jul will be
postponed to a later date.

We apologise for this late notice. The safety of our students is paramount and we will be doing a full
and thorough check of the classrooms during this period.

Thank you for your understanding.
