## * * * IMPORTANT SAFETY RECALL * * *

Safety Recall Notice 24S67 / NHTSA Recall 24V805

2023 Transit
This Notice Applies to Your Vehicle, Vehicle Identification Number (VIN):

This notice is sent to you in accordance with the National Traffic and Motor Vehicle Safety Act.
Ford Motor Company has decided that a defect which relates to motor vehicle safety exists in certain
2023-2024 Transit vehicles, including the VIN shown above.
We apologize for this situation and want to assure you that, with your assistance, we will correct this
condition. Our commitment, together with your dealer, is to provide you with the highest level of service
and support.

What is the issue? It is possible that incorrect bolts were installed on your vehicle during repairs
performed for Safety Recall 24S05, which may result in inadequate rear axle
bolt engagement.

What is the risk? Inadequate rear axle bolt engagement may lead to rear wheel lock-up or
possible separation of the wheel from the axle shaft, resulting in loss of vehicle
control or loss of vehicle motive power. In some cases, the vehicle may roll in
park if the parking brake is not applied. These conditions increase the risk of
crash or injury.

What will Ford and Parts are now available to repair your vehicle. Ford Motor Company has
your dealer do? authorized your dealer to inspect the rear axle, replace axle bolts, and as
needed, replace certain bearings or the full rear axle assembly free of charge
(parts and labor).

How long will it take? The time needed for this repair is less than one full day. However, due to
service scheduling requirements, your dealer may need your vehicle for a
longer period of time. In addition, your vehicle will require an inspection to
determine if parts need to be ordered.

What should you do? Please call your dealer without delay and request a service date for Recall
24S67. Provide the dealer with your VIN, which is printed near your name at
the beginning of this letter.
Ford has not issued instructions to stop driving your vehicle under this safety
recall. You should contact your dealer for an appointment to have your vehicle
remedied as soon as practicable.
If you do not already have a servicing dealer, you can access
ford.com/support for dealer addresses, maps, and driving instructions.
Ford Motor Company wants you to have this safety recall completed on your
vehicle. The vehicle owner is responsible for making arrangements to have
the work completed. Ford Motor Company can deny coverage for any vehicle
damage that may result from the failure to have this recall performed on a
timely basis. Therefore, please have this recall performed as soon as possible.
Please note: Federal law requires that any vehicle lessor receiving this
recall notice must forward a copy of this notice to the lessee within ten
days.

Este aviso se le envía de acuerdo con la Ley Nacional de Seguridad de Tránsito y Vehículos Motorizados de
los EE. UU.
Ford Motor Company ha decidido que existe un defecto relacionado con la seguridad de los vehículos
motorizados en ciertos vehículos Transit 2023-2024, incluido el VIN que se muestra arriba.
Lamentamos esta situación y deseamos asegurarle que, con su ayuda, corregiremos el problema. Nuestro
compromiso, junto con el de su concesionario, es ofrecerle servicio y apoyo de alto nivel.
¿Cuál es el Es posible que se hayan instalado pernos incorrectos en su vehículo durante las
problema? reparaciones realizadas para la Campaña de seguridad 24S05, lo que puede
provocar un acoplamiento inadecuado del tornillo del eje trasero.
¿Qué riesgo existe? Un acoplamiento inadecuado del perno del eje trasero puede provocar el bloqueo
de la rueda trasera o la posible separación de la rueda de la semiflecha, lo que
resulta en la pérdida de control del vehículo o la pérdida de potencia motriz del
vehículo. En algunos casos, el vehículo puede moverse en posición de
estacionamiento si no se aplica el freno de estacionamiento. Estas condiciones
aumentan el riesgo de accidente o lesiones.

tomará? embargo, debido a los requisitos de planificación de servicio, es posible que su
concesionario tarde un poco más. Además, su vehículo requerirá una inspección
para determinar si es necesario pedir piezas.

a cabo la campaña 24S67. Proporcione el VIN a su concesionario, el cual está
impreso debajo de sus datos al comienzo de esta carta.

programar una cita para solucionar este problema lo más pronto posible.

ford.com/support para conocer las direcciones de los concesionarios, ver mapas y

necesarios para llevar a cabo el trabajo. Ford Motor Company puede negar la
cobertura en caso de que el vehículo hubiese sufrido daños por no haber realizado
la campaña de manera oportuna. Por lo tanto, le solicitamos que realice esta
campaña lo antes posible.
