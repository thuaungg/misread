**WEIGHTED ASSESSMENT AT BOWEN SECONDARY – SECONDARY 4/5**

1. Aligned with MOE’s Learning for Life movement to provide more time for deep learning, reduce
overemphasis on academic results and promote student well-being, Bowen Secondary adopts an
assessment framework that balances alternative and traditional modes of assessment.

2. Please see below for details on how Term Assessments are conducted in Bowen Secondary School:

• All Pen and Paper tests (PPs) will be conducted during curriculum time (i.e. a lesson for the
relevant subject, unless otherwise stated). The duration of each PP will typically be 45 min, with
minor differences based on individual subject requirements.

• Please refer to the **Annex** included at the end of this letter for the TA Schedule. To enable
students to take ownership of their learning, subject teachers will communicate the assessment
dates for PPs and coverage of each assessment directly with students via channels such as
Google Classroom and Class Notice Boards. Students are expected to keep track of their own
assessment dates e.g. through Google Calendar (which they can share with parents as well).

3. To help students manage their workload, there will be no more than 2 assessments conducted for
students per day. This includes PPs and other school-wide assessments (e.g. oral examinations).

4. Assessment Format and Weightings
• In Terms 1 to 2, students’ learning in each subject is assessed through TAs, which are aligned
to established national standards and carefully spread across a ‘TA Window’ in Weeks 5 to 8
(Term 1) or Weeks 3 to 7 (Term 2) to manage the demands on students and enable them to
continue enjoying learning during lessons.
• In Term 3, students will sit for their Preliminary Examinations (N-Level: Term 3 Weeks 5-6; O-
Level: Term 3 Weeks 8-10).
• For Secondary 4/5 students, TAs and Preliminary Examinations will be pen and paper
assessments (or E-exams where applicable) and aligned to national examination requirements
and format. This helps prepare students progressively for the rigour and demands of the national
examination papers that they will be sitting for at the end of the year.
• Please note that the weightings of the TA and exams are as follows:

Level **TA1 TA2 Prelim Exams** Term 4
Term 1 Term 2 Term 3
Sec 4/5 15% 15% 70% N/O Levels

5. We strongly advise all students to take their TAs and Preliminary Exams seriously as these will
provide important information about their subject mastery and learning gaps. Any absences **must be** supported by a Medical Certificate or reason deemed valid by the school. Absences deemed as
valid will be reflected accordingly in your child/ward’s results slip.

6. Termly result slips will be issued to keep parents/guardians informed of their child’s/ward’s progress.
Form Teachers may meet or speak with parents/guardians of selected students.

7. Should you need any clarification, please feel free to contact your child/ward’s Form Teacher. We
seek your support in ensuring that your child/ward prepares well for all assessments.
