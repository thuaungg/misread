**Renovation of School Canteen**

The school canteen will undergo renovation works from 9 Mar 2026 and will continue through the 1-week
March school holidays. We would like to inform you of the temporary canteen operational arrangements
that will be in place from Mon, 9 Mar to Thu, 12 Mar 2026 **.**

During this period, the following arrangements will be in place:

**Guidelines Details**
Food Food will still be available at the canteen and will be packed in styrofoam lunch
boxes with plastic spoons and forks. Plates will not be used during this period.

Menu Restrictions Soup-based dishes will not be available.

Eating Areas Students will consume their meals at the basketball court and other designated
eating areas. The canteen seating area will not be available.

Canteen Operating Monday to Thursday: Open until **3:45 pm**
Hours

School Activities All CCAs and consultations will continue as per normal.

We seek your understanding and support as these arrangements are implemented. Please be assured
that the school will continue to ensure the well-being and safety of our students during this period.

The newly-furnished school canteen is expected to be in operation with effect from Term 2, Mon, 23 Mar
2026.
