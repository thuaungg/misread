**Important Information… Key Reminders**

7. Photography and Videography. We would like to reiterate that throughout the course
of the year, photograph(s) or video image(s) of you and your child may be captured at all
school events and activities. We may use and publish such photographs and/or video
recordings in school publications, website, social media channels and other official school
communication channels. For clarifications, please feel free to contact the school.

8. Attendance. Any absence from school must be supported by a medical certificate (MC)
or a letter (including email) from parents. However, the school accepts only up to 5 parental
letters or emails per semester to excuse absences. We kindly request that parents avoid taking
your child out of school for extended family holidays during term time, as this helps us
safeguard the protected curriculum time for the year. Please note that you will receive a
notification via Parents Gateway (PG) if your child is absent. You will be able to submit the
required MC or parental letter via PG.

9. Punctuality. Students are expected to attend school punctually every day. Punctuality
is an important value that we aim to develop in our students, and we encourage parents to
partner with us in emphasising its importance. All students must report to their designated
assembly areas by 7.30 a.m. Students who are not seated by this time will be considered
late. Disciplinary consequences may be implemented for students identified to be recalcitrant
latecomers.

10. Communications. All official communications with parents will be conducted through
Parents Gateway (PG). We encourage all parents to regularly check the notifications sent on
PG to stay informed of important information and, where necessary, provide timely consent
for your child's participation in school-organised programmes and activities. We urge parents
to refrain from contacting their child's teachers after 5.30 p.m. on school days, and on non-
school days and weekends, unless the matter requires urgent attention.

13. As part of the TDD commemoration, the school will also participate in ESR 26 on 11

14. Fire Evacuation Drill. The school will dovetail the conduct of fire evacuation drill with
ESR 2026. This is necessary to familiarise your child with the evacuation procedures. The
main objective of the fire drill is to safeguard lives by ensuring a systematic and orderly
evacuation in the event of an actual emergency. Additionally, fire drills help prevent panic and
familiarise students and teachers with the necessary actions during a fire emergency.

15. Student Well-being. Communicable diseases such as influenza A continue to pose a
threat to the well-being of our students, hence the need to remain vigilant. The threat to student
well-being is real as an infectious cluster could potentially develop from a single source, given
the large gatherings and confined spaces in schools.

16. If your child is unwell, please ensure he/she stays at home and seeks medical attention.
If your child is recovering from a respiratory-related illness and must attend school, please
encourage them to exercise social responsibility by wearing a mask. Should your child become
unwell or develop flu-like symptoms while at school, we will pre-arrange with parent to send
your child home. We appreciate your support and will continue to work closely with you. Let
us all do our part to exercise personal and social responsibility to maintain a safe environment.

17. Road Safety. We seek parents' assistance in emphasising the importance of road
safety to your child as he/she resumes his/her routine of travelling to and from school. Please
remind your child to always exercise caution and care on the road. Specifically, he/she should:
• use pedestrian crossings;
• practise the Kerb Drill when crossing the road;
• obey traffic signals;
• avoid crossing in front of stationary vehicles or between vehicles, as drivers may
not see them;
• refrain from running, playing, or using personal devices (such as mobile phones)
when crossing the road; and
• use footpaths and walkways instead of walking on the side of the road.

**Miscellaneous…**

32. Toilet Revamp. The school is scheduled to carry out Additions and Alterations (A&A)
works for toilets (more than 20 years in service) located at the classroom block, Indoor Sports
Hall (ISH), canteen and GO block. The A&A works will be carried out in phases to minimise
the inconveniences to the students and staff, starting March 2026 until 1 st quarter of 2027.
Specific arrangements and directional signages will be implemented to guide students to the
designated toilets, so that their daily routines and well-being are adequately supported
throughout the renovation period.

33. Replacement of Synthetic Turf. Scheduled to complete by March, a new artificial
synthetic field with better shock absorbing capability will enhance the safety during PE, CCA
and games. The synthetic turf will overlay a new and better drainage network underneath to
address the ponding issue previously experienced at the field itself and the adjacent
sheltered basketball court during heavy downpour.
