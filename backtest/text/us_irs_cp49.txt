**Protection from your spouse’s** When you file a joint tax return, you may be able to prevent some or all
**debt** of your overpayment from paying liabilities for which your spouse (or
former spouse) is responsible. For example, if some or all of your
overpayment from a joint return has been (or will be) applied to pay
your spouse’s past-due income taxes, health coverage shared
responsibility payment, or other debt (child support, spousal support,
student loans), you may be entitled to relief as an injured spouse. If
you’re eligible, you may be entitled to a refund for your share of an
overpayment that’s been (or will be) applied to your spouse’s debt. For
more information, or to submit a claim, go to www.irs.gov and download
the Injured Spouse Allocation (Form 8379) or call 1-800-829-3676 to
request a copy.
