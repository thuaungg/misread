• If you received interest or dividend income and you don’t file
your return or pay all taxes due, you could become subject to
backup withholding. This means IRS will notify your payers
(banks, etc.) to withhold a percentage of the payments you
receive for dividends and interest. The payers will send the
money to the IRS and you may claim it as a withholding credit
on your federal income tax return.

information, visit www.irs.gov/offers.
