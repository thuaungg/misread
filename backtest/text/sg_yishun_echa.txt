**EDUSAVE CHARACTER AWARD 2024 NOMINATIONS**

Dear Parents/Guardian

1. The Edusave Character Award (ECHA) was introduced by Ministry of Education in
2012 to recognise students who demonstrate exemplary character and outstanding
personal qualities through their behaviour and actions. This award aligns with our
school’s efforts to develop morally upright and caring students who contribute to the
nation.

2. ECHA awardees must satisfy the following eligibility criteria:

a) Awardees must be Singaporeans

b) Awardees must, on a consistent basis, demonstrate good character and values.

3. Nominations can be submitted by teachers and other key stakeholders (including
parents) who interact with the students.

4. Shortlisted nominees will undergo further rounds of evaluation, including an interview
with the panel.

5. We invite parents to submit nominations if you believe your child is deserving of this
award. Nominations can be submitted online via the link found on the school’s
website: https://yishunsec.moe.edu.sg

6. Kindly submit your nominations by 6 September 2024, 5:00 PM.

Thank you.

Mrs Regina Lee
Principal
