**Important Information… Safety, Emergency Preparedness & Student Well-Being**

4. Emergency Preparedness Drills. The Attendance and Temperature Taking Exercise
was conducted on 9 July. During this exercise, students were responsible for taking their own
temperature using their personal Oral Digital Thermometer (ODT). This exercise maintains
our school's preparedness to reactivate the attendance and temperature taking regime should
the need arise, while educating students about personal health responsibility.

5. As part of our emergency preparedness, the school plans to conduct an evacuation
drill where our school population will practise the Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) for a
school lockdown exercise, simulating an intruder incident. This will be followed by a fire-related
scenario where the school population will assemble at the designated assembly area (AA).
Your child's Form Teachers will provide detailed information about the drill via Parents
Gateway (PG) in due course.

potential. We develop this through various engagements focusing on core values,
competencies and student support. Our approach encompasses:

**a. Nurturing a Growth Mindset: A Foundation for Resilience**
Our teaching and learning programmes are built on the cornerstone of growth mindset,
emphasising that abilities can be developed through dedication and effort. This
mindset encourages students to view challenges and setbacks as opportunities for
growth rather than indicators of fixed ability, enabling them to better cope with stress,
persevere through difficulties and bounce back from setbacks.

**b. FTGP: Equipping Students with Social and Emotional Competencies**
The Form Teacher Guidance Period (FTGP) plays a vital role in supporting students'
mental resilience. Through structured lessons and activities, students develop
essential social and emotional competencies, including emotional management,
positive relationships and coping strategies. FTGP provides a platform for addressing
specific well-being issues, ensuring our students are well-equipped to navigate life's
challenges.

**c. One-to-One Interaction: Building Rapport and Support**
Our twice-weekly One-to-One Interaction sessions provide dedicated time for teachers
to build strong relationships with their students. These sessions create a safe and
supportive environment that allows teachers to better understand their students' needs
and concerns, fostering the trust and security essential for students' mental well-being.

16. Physical Well-Being. Physical well-being is a key domain of student development. We
recognise that good physical health promotes better memory retention and increased energy
levels. At school, we nurture our students' physical well-being through various activities,
including Physical Education lessons and structured programmes such as SwimSafer, Co-
Curricular Activities and Outdoor Education programmes, each tailored to different age groups.
We are pleased to observe positive trends in our students' physical well-being and hope
parents will continue to partner with us in supporting your child's physical development. Thank
you for your support.

Singapore Youth Festival 2025 Art Exhibition this March with two teams of aspiring young

the remarkable potential of student collaboration.
