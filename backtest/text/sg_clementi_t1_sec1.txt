**Ensuring Smooth Transition to Sec 1**
3 Your child/ward’s transition to secondary school is an important milestone, and your support will
play a key role in helping him/her settle confidently into new routines, expectations, and subjects. We look
forward to working closely with you in this journey and would like to suggest some ways in which you can
support your child/ward at home while strengthening our home – school partnership:
• Take time to talk with your child/ward about his/her school day. Where possible, focus on positive
experiences to help nurture excitement and confidence about school.
• Pay attention to your child/ward’s strengths and interests, and observe what motivates and
engages him/her.
• Create a conducive home environment that encourages reading and supports your child/ward in
completing his/her homework independently.
• Guide your child/ward in managing his/her time effectively, and encourage a healthy, balanced
lifestyle with adequate rest, play, and family time.

1

• As part of the learning process, your child/ward will inevitably encounter challenges and
frustrations. Reassure him/her that making mistakes is a natural part of learning, and support
him/her in persevering through difficulties. Encourage your child/ward to ask questions in class
and to seek help from teachers when needed.
• Support your child/ward in building positive relationships with peers by encouraging cooperation,
empathy, and consideration for others.

**Blended Learning & CTSS Self-Directed Learning Days**
12 MOE first announced the implementation of Blended Learning and regularly scheduled Home-Based
Learning days for secondary schools in 2020. In brief, Blended Learning means our students will have
opportunities to learn using both online and offline approaches, both in class as well as on home-based
learning days. When in class, blended learning is aided by students’ personal learning devices (PLDs).

13 Blended Learning will also occur on Home-Based Learning days, which CTSS refers to as Self-Directed
Learning (SDL) Days. SDL Days aim to provide students with more opportunities to learn at their own pace
and to be empowered to take charge of their learning. There will also be an emphasis on Student-Initiated
Learning, whereby dedicated time and space will be provided to allow students to pursue their own interests
and learn outside of the curriculum.

14 In CTSS, SDL Days is a regular feature in our students’ learning experiences, spanning Terms 1 to 3 of
a school year for Sec 1 to 3. SDL Days are scheduled on **Even Week Fridays for Term 1 and Term 2** .

15 To prepare our students for their learning on SDL Days, **the first SDL Day will be held in school on Friday 16 January 2026, from 7.30 a.m. to 12.40 p.m** . Sec 1 students will need to bring their writing materials
and learning materials for the SDL-featured subjects e.g. textbook, notes, assignments. They will learn and
apply learning strategies, study skills, time management, and self-regulating strategies such as goal setting,
action planning, monitoring and evaluating their progress.

16 From the second SDL day (Friday 30 January 2026) onwards, your child/ward need not report to
school on SDL Days. Sec 1 students will use their own devices on SDL Days for the time being until they
receive their PLDs, which is expected to be at the end of Term 1. The school will reach out to Sec 1 students
who might need assistance with learning devices on SDL Days. Students are expected to exercise their learner
agency in planning their learning schedule on these days which would include the subjects mentioned below.
They are expected to complete the learning tasks and assignments via e-Learning portals. The instructions

**Total Defence Week 2026 (9 to 13 February 2026)**
23 The focus for Total Defence Day 2026 “Together We Keep Singapore Strong”, will highlight
Singaporean’s readiness and resilience in the face of crises and challenges. To encourage our students to
develop and value this resilience, we will be participating in the Exercise SG Ready 2026 (ESR26) from 9 to
13 February 2026.

24 Exercise SG Ready (ESR) is a nationwide exercise to strengthen Singapore’s preparedness and
resilience against crises and disruptions. Simulations of possible crises and disruptions that may happen in
Singapore enable students to learn to respond in a calm, resilient and responsible way, look out for each
other, and be prepared for future threats and challenges. It will also be an occasion to rally our students to
Connect and Contribut e through acts of Care . Through the shared experience of possible disruption, students
will have the opportunity to listen to others’ stories, reflect on their own experiences of resilience and
adaptability, and cultivate the Will to Act in order to Contribute positively to their lives and the community
around them. More information on ESR will be shared with parents through Parents Gateway.
