**School Rules YSS Handphone Policy**

Mobile phones must be switched off during school hours including recess and lunch break, and may
only be used after school. Students are to place their mobile phones in the handphone pouch at the
front of the classroom before the start of the lesson, and will only collect them back after the end of
the lessons.

Mobile phones must not be used during consultation, enrichment programmes, learning journeys, CCA
activities and any other school functions and activities unless authorised by teachers.
Parents/guardians are to call the General Office at 68767129 if they wish to contact their child/ward.

Students are not allowed to use their phone’s camera or video functions, or to access the internet
during curriculum hours without the teacher’s permission.

**WE SEEK, WE STRIVE, WE SOAR**

Mobile phones must not be brought into the examination room or hall during weighted assessment,
school examination and national examination. All mobile phones are to be switched off and kept in the
students’ school bags during weighted assessments and examinations.

Students are discouraged from bringing mobile phones to school. They are responsible for the
safekeeping of their mobile phone. Student’s mobile phone will be confiscated if he/she violates any
of the above guidelines.

**Leaving of School Premises**

Students are not allowed to leave the school premises before the commencement of any after-school
programmes such as CCA, consultations and Learning Journeys.

**Attire and Hairstyle**

Students should look neat and presentable at all times. There should be no outlandish hairstyles.

**Student Matters Secondary One Orientation Programme 2025**

The Secondary One Orientation Programme 2025 will take place from 2 Jan 2025 to 6 Jan 2025. The
theme of the programme is We Seek, Strive and Soar and it is jointly planned and run by the Student
Development Team (SDT) and the Student Leadership Committee. The orientation programme aims
to help our new Secondary One students ease into secondary school life as seamlessly as possible.

The engaging and purposeful activities facilitated by the Prefects and Peer Support Leaders will allow
our Secondary One students to learn more about the school and establish meaningful relationships
with their teachers and peers.

Students are allowed to wear half uniform or PE attire for these 3 days of orientation, and they are
reminded to bring their water bottles with them. Normal lessons will commence on Tue, 7 Jan 2025.

**Secondary One Parents Engagement Session**

The Secondary One Parents Engagement Session will be held on Fri, 10 Jan at 5 pm in the school
hall. The session will touch on Secondary One CCA selection, LEAPS, Full Subject-Based Banding,
and the Personalised Digital Learning Programme (PDLP). Parents would also get to meet their
child/ward’s form teachers.

A separate notification on Parents Gateway will be sent nearer the date to confirm your attendance as
light snacks will be provided. Parents are also encouraged to join their child for our CCA Open House.

Students who are offered More Demanding Level (MDL) subjects should have received their offer
letter via Parents Gateway. To share more about the demand and expectation of taking MDL subjects,
parents are invited to attend a short briefing at 4:30 pm in the LT@3 on Fri, 10 Jan.
