**2. SCHOOL ROUTINES 2.1 Attendance**
At Yu Neng Primary, we place strong emphasis on regular and punctual school attendance. We
believe that consistent attendance enables students to make optimal progress in developing
healthy habits, life skills, character, and academic learning.
Parents are kindly reminded **not** to take their children out of school during term time without valid
reasons, including for overseas travel. Should there be a need for leave during term time, parents
are required to submit a request for a Leave of Absence (LOA) in advance to the Principal through
the Form Teacher.
All absences from school must be supported by a medical certificate (MC) or a letter of excuse
from parents/guardians, which should be submitted via Parents Gateway. We appreciate your kind
understanding and cooperation in supporting your child’s learning and development.

**3.4 Primary 5 Project Work**
Project Work (PW) is a multidisciplinary learning experience that allows students to think
creatively and critically to apply the knowledge learnt in lessons in the real-world context to create
for good. Students will also reflect on and evaluate their work processes and learning attitudes
for continuous improvement. In Yu Neng, our PW takes on a blended approach where students
employ the effective use of technology to hone their research skills, communicate ideas and
collaborate with peers online. They would also harness the affordances of technology to engage
an authentic audience in the presentation of their work confidently and motivate their audience to
action in their causes.

There will be scheduled Home-Based Learning (HBL) Days for P3 to P5 during PW to provide
the opportunity and experience for students to demonstrate the necessary digital literacy skills
and dispositions as they work towards becoming self-directed and independent learners. On HBL
Days, students of the mentioned levels are expected to stay at home to engage in planned online
and offline learning activities.

Your child will also be provided with instructions on how to access their learning materials by
his/her teachers. Please make home care arrangements for your child if he/she has home-based
learning. Students enrolled in the school-based Student Care Centre (SCC) may attend from 1.30
p.m. on HBL days and please coordinate directly with Big Heart SCC on this.

This year, our P5 students will embark on a rice-planting journey to learn more about food
sustainability. The P5 HBL Day is scheduled for **Wednesday, 11 February 2026** and more
information will be shared in a separate letter. HBL information for P3 and P4 students will also
be shared separately in due course.

**4.5 Insurance for Students**

All students in government and government aided schools are covered under the Ministry of
Education's Group Personal Accident (MOE GPA) Insurance Plan for Students. The MOE GPA
insurance is provided at no cost to parents. This insurance covers students for accidents that may
occur during school hours, during school-related activities, and while students are travelling
directly to and from school, subject to the policy's claim limits. We strongly recommend that
students seek treatment at public healthcare institutions to ensure sufficient coverage under the
MOE GPA insurance scheme. As private hospitals charge significantly higher medical costs, there
may be more out-of-pocket expenses for parents/guardians as there are caps to the MOE GPA
insurance. Please note that all claims are subject to the policy terms and conditions. Please refer
to the attached detailed factsheet that outlines the specific coverage details, online claim
procedures, and important terms and conditions.
**4.6 Birthday Celebrations**

The school will not accept requests from parents to celebrate their children’s birthdays in school.
The guideline is put in place to prevent:
● Potential food safety and allergies incidents

● Unnecessary pressure upon students to get their parents to organise celebrations for them
