We hope you have had some quality time together with your child/ward during September

**2. Celebrating Learning, Culture and Community in Term 3**
In Term 3, our students embarked on a vibrant and memorable journey filled with enriching
experiences that nurtured both their hearts and minds. They proudly represented the school at
the National School Games and immersed themselves in the beauty of their linguistic roots
through the Mother Tongue Language Fortnight and the Tamil Language Cluster Festival.
Demonstrating remarkable compassion and generosity, students also rallied together for the
Bags of Love Food Donation Drive, making a heartfelt difference to those in the community.
National pride shone brightly as students came together to commemorate National Day and
Racial Harmony Day, deepening their appreciation for our shared Singapore story. Students
were further inspired through the Aesthetics programme, a captivating Cohort Learning
Journey through Museum-Based Learning, and an adventurous P4 Outdoor Education Day
Camp that built resilience and camaraderie.

In addition, at the Prefect Appreciation and Investiture 2026, we thanked our outgoing
senior prefects for their service and leadership and formally invested our new prefects, who
pledged to serve their schoolmates and lead with the GIVER 3 S values, in the presence of
supportive parents/guardians. We are grateful to every parent/guardian who made time to be
with us. This is a direction we intend to continue. We will again open our celebrations to
parents/guardians at our year-end celebrations in November, so that we can mark our students’
growth together with you. The term culminated in a warm and joyful Teachers’ Day Celebration,
where students expressed their heartfelt gratitude to the dedicated educators who guide and
inspire them every day.

**5. Term 4 GIVER 3 S Values – Integrity and Sincerity**
This term, we focus on the values of **Integrity** and **Sincerity** . We want our students to
understand that integrity means choosing to do what is right, even when no one is watching. It
is about being honest, taking responsibility for our choices, and having the courage to stand by
what is right. Sincerity reminds us to be genuine in our words and actions – to mean what we
say, keep our promises, and treat others with honesty and care. Through everyday choices
and interactions, we hope our students will grow to become young people who can be trusted 3
to do what is right and who relate to others with sincerity. We invite parents and guardians to e g

**9. Home-Based Learning (HBL) for P1 to P5**
To help our students grow into self-directed and future-ready learners, HBL days are
scheduled in Term 4. These sessions help build digital literacy, responsibility, and

environment. Please guide your child/ward to attend synchronous lessons and complete their
assignments punctually. You may refer to pages 38–43 of the Student Handbook for detailed
guidelines and online learning resources.

**11. Children’s Day Celebrations**

**12. Primary 6 Post-PSLE Activities & Transition to Secondary School Programme**
Following the completion of the PSLE, curriculum for Primary 6 cohort will continue for the
rest of the seven weeks as the students participate in the P6 Post-PSLE Activities and
Transition to Secondary School Programme. Attendance for the students is compulsory during
the post-PSLE period. The programme seeks to prepare students mentally and emotionally for
a smoother transition into the next phase of their education journey at the secondary level.
Through the various meaningful and enriching learning experiences, students will be equipped
with life skills that encourage self-awareness, independence and confidence.

As part of the Primary 6 Post-PSLE Activities & Transition to Secondary School Programme,
our Education and Career Guidance (ECG) Day 2026 will be held on 3 November 2026
(Tuesday). Primary 6 students will research careers across various industries and put together
exhibits to share their learning with their level mates and Primary 5 juniors. Through this, we
hope to broaden students’ awareness of what lies beyond their school years and encourage
them to think about their interests, strengths and future possibilities. Each year, our Parent
Support Group (PSG) has been a valued partner in making ECG Day a meaningful experience
by connecting us with parents from diverse professions to share their career journeys and
experiences with our graduating students. We are grateful for their continued support in
enriching our students’ learning and for their part in nurturing future-ready citizens.

More details will be shared with the students in due course.

**Annex B EXAMINATION REGULATIONS FOR ALL STUDENTS**
(Source: 2026 Primary School Leaving Examination - Examination Rules and Regulations for
Candidates Page 9 – 17)
You must comply with the following regulations during the examination:
• You must not attempt to cheat or assist in cheating.
• **You should always face the front. Do not look around during your examination,** as
this may be perceived as an attempt to cheat **.**
• **You must not communicate (verbally or non-verbally) with other candidates** before
and during the examination, and before you are released from your examination venue.
• You must not have in your possession:
a) any unauthorised electronic, smart, computerised or communication devices* in the
examination venue (e.g. examination room, quarantine room, waiting room).
* Examples of unauthorised devices include, but are not limited to, mobile phone,
camera, tablet, pocket PC, Personal Digital Assistant, digital diary, ebook, wireless
earbuds/earphones, smart watch, fitness/step tracker, smart glasses, storage
device, audio recorder/player and gaming device.
b) any unapproved printed dictionaries/ electronic dictionaries or calculators within the
examination venue (e.g. examination room/hall) for papers that allow the use of
dictionaries or calculators.
c) any unauthorised materials* within the examination venue. All stationery/
belongings taken into the examination venue (e.g. pencil case, calculator(s),
mathematical set, ruler) must not have unauthorised notes/information written on
them before or during the examination.
*Examples of unauthorised materials include, but are not limited to, non-standard
mathematical instruments e.g. model rulers, conversion table/ mathematical formula
sheet enclosed in or printed on the mathematical instrument box/calculator, writing
papers, books, sticky notes, reference materials or notes.
d) your bags or other unauthorised personal belongings in the examination premises.
You must place them outside the examination venue.
• Surrender any unauthorised devices, equipment, materials and personal belongings to
the invigilator immediately if you have brought them into the examination room.
• If you are not feeling well or need to go to the toilet urgently, raise your hand and
remain seated. The examination personnel will direct you to a rest area or toilet.
• **No eating is allowed** in the examination venues (e.g. examination room/hall,
quarantine room, waiting room).

If you cheat, attempt to cheat, assist in cheating, misbehave or do not follow the
examination regulations during the examination, you may:
• be banned from taking the examination
• be expelled from the examination venue (e.g. examination room/hall)
• have your examination results terminated or be imposed with a grade penalty
