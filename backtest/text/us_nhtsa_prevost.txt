## IMPORTANT SAFETY RECALL

This notice applies to your vehicle(s), see VIN list below.

Dear Customer,

This notice is sent to you in accordance with the National Traffic and Motor Vehicle Safety Act.

Prevost Car US Inc. has decided that certain 2025 Volvo 9700 passenger coaches fail to
conform to Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standard No. 217 - Bus emergency exits and
window retention and release.

**DEFECT DESCRIPTION**
The emergency exits on the concerned vehicle may not comply with FMVSS217 S5.5.1
because certain emergency window exit identification and operating instructions labels are
potentially not adhered properly to the vehicle which could cause the label to peel off easily
or become easily damaged.

**SAFETY RISK**
In an emergency evacuation situation, a non-compliance with FMVSS 217S5.5.1 could
increase passenger evacuation time and the risk of injury.

**IDENTIFICATION OF ANY WARNING THAT CAN PRECEDE**
In some cases, peeling of the emergency exit label can be identified.

**REIMBURSEMENT OF PRE-NOTIFICATION REMEDIES**
When a Prevost vehicle is affected by a safety recall and the owner/purchaser (claimant)

expense prior to receiving the manufacturer notification of the safety recall, Prevost Car (US)
