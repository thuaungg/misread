three drop-off lots at the designated drop-off point in front of the school concourse (see Figure

position your vehicle within the drop-off lots

lots will also facilitate two concurrent drop-

long tailbacks. For safety reasons, please
ensure that your child alights from the left Figure 1 – Drop-Off Lots

motorcycle lots to discourage driver from performing
three-point turn at the handicap and motorcycle lots. We Loading & Unloading Bay

other road users. Dropping off at the loading & unloading
bay is also strongly discouraged. We thank parents for
your cooperation and understanding.

**Student Matters**

6. Students’ Punctuality. The school seeks support from parents to advocate the
importance of punctuality. All students should report to their designated assembly areas
(classrooms, Multi-Purpose Hall, or Indoor Sports Hall) before flag raising commences at 7.30
am. Arriving on time ensures children do not miss morning activities and prevents classroom
disruptions. Punctuality also instils valuable life skills such as responsibility and respect for
others.

7. Creating A Vibrant Environment. We continue to enhance the learning environment for
our students. The refurbished parade square now provides a purposeful space for students to
learn, play and grow (see Figure 3). The hopscotch play area, modular courts and
running/cycling track offer greater opportunities for students to stay active through structured
activities and free play. Students have been briefed on play safety, emphasising care for self
and others.

Figure 3 – A More Vibrant Parade Square

8. Library Fiesta 2025. We hope that the Library Fiesta 2025 has promoted reading and
broadened students' exposure to popular stories and characters, thereby strengthening their
appreciation of literature both in and beyond the classroom. Through engaging experiences
such as the Readers' Theatre, student book talks, character dress-up, and interactive recess
and classroom activities, students were immersed in the world of stories and characters (see
Figure 4). These activities not only created a joyful and vibrant atmosphere around books, but
also encouraged students to explore new genres, express their creativity, and share their
reflections with their peers.

9. Clean Plate Campaign. The Clean Plate Campaign took place from 17 to 19
September, aimed at educating students on food sustainability and reducing food waste. The
campaign featured (a) Green Leaders sharing insights during Positive Minutes to encourage
students to finish their meals and (b) an assembly programme on food sustainability. Students
were encouraged to finish their food at recess, and we hope that the campaign will foster
mindful eating habits and greater awareness of food sustainability amongst our students (see
Figure 5). We encourage parents to reinforce these habits at home by discussing the
importance of not wasting food and modelling mindful eating practices during family meals.

10. Academic Support Programme for Selected P6 Students. We continued to provide
support to selected P6 students in the lead-up to the PSLE, including face-to-face revision
lessons in the school during the pre-PSLE study break from 22 to 24 September. Our P6
teachers maintained open communication platforms to assist students who sought
clarifications and had doubts during their home revision. All the best to our graduating cohort!

11. Building Social Competence. We build social competence in our students, anchored
on the values of care and integrity, through Form Teacher Guidance Period (FTGP) and CCE
lessons. FTGP and CCE lessons explicitly teach teamwork, empathy, and responsible
decision-making, whilst CCAs provide authentic opportunities to practise leadership and
collaboration. Our teaching and learning approach, which comprises teachers designing group
work, discussions, and project-based learning where students share roles, communicate
respectfully, and problem-solve together, further strengthens these skills. Teachers and staff
also capitalise on teachable moments resulting from incidents and observations to inculcate
social competence.
