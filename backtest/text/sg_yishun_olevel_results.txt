**Release of the 2025 GCE O - Level Examination Results**

The 2025 GCE O - Level examination results will be released on Wednesday, 14 January 2026.

Students will be allowed to enter the school from 1:30 pm onwards and are expected to be seated in
the SPACE (Level 3) by 1:50 pm.

All students receiving their results are expected to be in proper attire (full-uniform or half-uniform) and
proper grooming. Parents accompanying their child are to sign in at the guard house.

Students who are unwell should seek medical attention and not return to school to collect their results.

Students who are unable to collect their results in person are to appoint a proxy to collect a physical
copy of their results from the school. Proxies are required to produce the relevant documents for the
school’s verification when collecting the results.

Best wishes to all students collecting their results.

Keep well and stay safe.
